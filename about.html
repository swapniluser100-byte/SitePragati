About SitePragati

We build affordable, no-nonsense websites for small businesses — cafés, shops, and local services — with online ordering, UPI payments, and enquiry handling built in from day one. No hosting fees, no waiting months.

What we build

  • Website design & build

    A custom-designed site for your business — not a generic template — covering your menu or services, photos, and contact details.

  • Online ordering & payments

    Customers browse, order, and pay by UPI directly — no transaction fees, no separate app to manage.

  • Enquiries, organized automatically

    Every message and order lands in your email and a spreadsheet you already know how to use — no new software to learn.

  • Ongoing support

    Menu changes, new photos, small fixes — usually turned around within a day.
