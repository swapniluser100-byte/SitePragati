About us

About SitePragati

We build affordable, no-nonsense websites for small businesses — cafés, shops, and local services — with online ordering, UPI payments, and enquiry handling built in from day one. No hosting fees, no waiting months.

What we build

  • Website design & build

    A custom-designed site for your business — not a generic template — covering your menu or services, photos, and contact details.

  • Online ordering & payments

    Customers browse, order, and pay by UPI directly — no transaction fees, no separate app to manage.

  • Enquiries, organized automatically

    Every message and order lands in your email and a spreadsheet you already know how to use — no new software to learn.

  • Ongoing support

    Menu changes, new photos, small fixes — usually turned around within a day.

  • Native mobile apps

    When your business is ready to go beyond the web, we build native Android & iOS apps — connected to the same ordering, payments, and enquiry system as your website, so your data stays in one place.

Native apps for small businesses

A website gets you found. An app keeps you in your customers' pockets — with repeat orders, push notifications, and a faster experience than a browser can offer.

  • Built for how your customers already shop

    A dedicated app on their home screen for reordering, checking order status, and paying — no browser tabs, no re-entering details every time.

  • One system, not two

    Your app runs on the same backend as your website and admin console — orders, payments, and customer records stay in sync automatically.

  • Push notifications built in

    Let customers know about order updates, offers, and reopenings the moment they happen, instead of hoping they check their inbox.

  • Launched and maintained by us

    We handle the Play Store & App Store listings, updates, and ongoing maintenance — you focus on running your business.
